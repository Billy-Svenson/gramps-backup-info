# Privacy Policy — Gramps Backups

Gramps Backups is a personal backup tool operated by the owner
of the Billy-Svenson GitHub account.

## Google Drive access

The tool uses the owner's authorization to create, read, verify,
and delete backup files in its designated Google Drive backup
folder. It does not intentionally access unrelated Drive files.

## Data and storage

Backup archives may contain private family-tree records, media,
and account data. Copies are stored on the owner's home server,
router, and authorized Google Drive account.

OAuth credentials are stored privately on the owner's computer
or server. They are not published on this website.

## Use and sharing

Google user data is used only to maintain and restore personal
backups. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train
AI models. Backups are not intentionally shared with other users.

## Retention and control

Backup copies are retained according to the owner's configured
retention policy. Some baseline copies may be retained indefinitely.
The owner can delete backups and revoke the tool's access through
Google Account permissions.

## Contact

For questions, contact the operator through the GitHub profile:
https://github.com/Billy-Svenson

Do not include private family information or credentials in
public GitHub messages.
